 XXCOUNTRYXX  national Digital Decade strategic roadmap

 XXCOUNTRYXX  submitted an adjustment to its national roadmap in January 2025, following the 2024 recommendations. The adjustment clarified the national approach, updated 5G coverage targets, and refined the measure set, without fundamentally revising the strategic directions. It also improved alignment with the Digital Decade objectives, enhanced stakeholder consultations, and updated links to policy.

 Targets:  XXCOUNTRYXX  partially addressed the recommendation to raise ambition. However, no fully new measures were introduced.  Digital Rights and Principles: Mapping of measures to the Digital Decade objectives and the European Declaration on Digital Rights and Principles was improved.  Consultation: The stakeholder consultation process was enhanced, including a kick-off conference in September 2024 and continuous coordination with relevant state bodies and associations, such as the  XXCOUNTRYXX  Employers' Association and the  XXCOUNTRYXX  Chamber of Economy.

While  XXCOUNTRYXX  adjusted roadmap covers the majority of Digital Decade targets, the absence of a target for edge node deployment highlights an important gap. Overall, the  XXCOUNTRYXX  roadmap includes 31 measures with a budget of EUR 635 million (equivalent to 0.74% of GDP).

Measures and budget in national roadmap

 XXCOUNTRYXX  adjustment improves the structure and clarity of the roadmap. The strengthened measure descriptions, enhanced mapping to objectives, and update of 5G targets are positive developments.
