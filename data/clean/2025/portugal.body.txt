With its Atlantic coastline and two outermost regions (Azores and Madeira), submarine cables are important digital infrastructures to ensure the security and sovereignty of  XXCOUNTRYXX  and the EU. Its deployment, supported in 2024 with EUR 14.3 million from the Connecting Europe Facility (CEF), will contribute these goals while simultaneously: providing high-speed data-transfer capabilities and secure and reliable communication channels and reducing the dependence of the outermost regions on non-European countries for critical digital infrastructure.
