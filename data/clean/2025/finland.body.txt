Citizens are particularly confident about getting freedom of assembly and of association in the digital environment and getting access to safe and privacy-friendly digital technologies (76%, above the EU average of 59% and 55% respectively). They are most worried that their right to a safe digital environment and content for children and young people is not well protected (57%, above the EU average of 48%).
